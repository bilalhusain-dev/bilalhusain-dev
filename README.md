> **Production-grade, open-source, multi-tenant Identity & Access Management (IAM) engine engineered as a self-hosted, high-performance alternative to WorkOS, Auth0, and Clerk with zero per-user licensing fees.**

* **Institutional SDLC:** Engineered following the complete **18-Step Production Engineering Lifecycle** with complete [PRD](https://github.com/bilalhusain-dev/enterprise-authentication-saas/blob/main/docs/01_BRD_AND_PRD.md), [Architecture ERD](https://github.com/bilalhusain-dev/enterprise-authentication-saas/blob/main/docs/03_SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md), [Security Audit](https://github.com/bilalhusain-dev/enterprise-authentication-saas/blob/main/docs/07_SECURITY_AND_COMPLIANCE.md), and [CI/CD Automation](https://github.com/bilalhusain-dev/enterprise-authentication-saas/blob/main/.github/workflows/ci.yml).
